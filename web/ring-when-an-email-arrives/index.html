HOW IT WORKS

How to make your phone ring when an email arrives

With an alarm for your email. You say which email you're waiting for, close your inbox and get on with your day; when that email lands, the phone rings. That is Email Alarm, and below is how it works on each phone.

A notification lasts a second.

An alarm rings until you stop it.

On AndroidAndroid

It rings like a real alarm clock: full screen, over the Lock Screen.

  • You create the alarm with the sender you're expecting, a word in the subject, or both.
  • It rings with the phone on silent and in Do Not Disturb too, if you grant that permission.
  • It doesn't connect to your email account and never asks for your password. Works with Gmail, Outlook, Samsung Email, Yahoo and more, across all your accounts at once.
  • Each alarm has its days, hours and tone, and a switch so it's on only when you need it.

On iPhoneiPhone

The notification arrives first and the alarm rings when you open it. iOS doesn't let an app take over the screen on its own.

  • You connect Gmail or Outlook through Google or Microsoft, without typing your password into Email Alarm.
  • You create the alarm with the sender, a word in the subject, or both, and choose whether one condition has to match or both.
  • Open the notification and the alarm rings until you stop it, showing who wrote, the subject, and which alarm matched.
  • Each alarm has its days, hours and tone, and you can snooze it for 1, 5 or 10 minutes.
