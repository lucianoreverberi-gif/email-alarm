Your email never leaves your phone.

Email Alarm reads the notifications your email app already shows and checks them right there, on your phone. It never logs into your email account and never sends your email anywhere. Its only connection is to Google Play, to check your subscription. No accounts, no ads, no tracking.

Good to know

What it needs to work, stated plainly.

  • Your email app has to be on the phone, with notifications on. If an account stops syncing, there's no notification to hear.
  • Emails that land in spam don't create a notification, so they can't ring either. For senders you don't know yet — an immigration office, a government agency — create a filter on the Gmail website with a word that will be in the email, and check “Never send it to Spam”. It works even if you don't know the address.
  • Outlook and some other apps only show the sender's name, not the address. For those, use the name or a keyword — the app tells you when a rule can't match.
  • Android only. iPhone doesn't let one app read another app's notifications, so this approach isn't possible there.
