Which phone?

Email Alarm works a different way on each one, so the answers are different too. Pick yours and go straight to what applies.

Help for Android

It rings full screen, even with the phone on silent. It reads your mail app's notifications and never connects to your account.

Permissions, battery, spam, schedules and cancelling.

Help for iPhone

A notification arrives and the alarm rings when you open it. It connects your Gmail or Outlook account and watches the inbox.

The two steps, the connected account, schedules and cancelling.
