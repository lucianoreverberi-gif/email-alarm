Good to know

What is different on iPhone, explained clearly.

  • With the screen locked, the tone lasts 24 seconds and respects iOS silent mode, Focus, volume and permissions.
  • Tap the notification to open the email details. A fullscreen view does not open automatically over the lock screen.
  • Internet is required. Delivery is not guaranteed: this is not an emergency service.
  • The beta supports one installation per connected account. Gmail may require renewing test authorization.
