Three steps. Your own rules.

1

Connect your account

Gmail or personal Hotmail/Outlook, using the provider’s authorization. Their mail apps do not need to be installed.

2

Choose what matches

Sender, a word or phrase in the subject. Choose Either or Both and a 24-second tone.

3

See why it rang

Open the alert to see the sender, subject and matching alarm. Manage your accounts and review activity.

How it sounds on iPhone

With the screen locked, an alert uses a 24-second tone and respects iPhone volume, silent mode, Focus and notification permissions. Inside the app, sound can repeat until you tap Stop. Tapping a notification opens its details; a custom fullscreen does not open automatically over the lock screen.

Internet is required. Delivery is not guaranteed and this is not an emergency service.

Privacy on iOS

Sender and subject. No body or attachments.

Unlike Android, iOS uses a server to check your rules. Connection credentials and alert details are stored encrypted. You can pause alerts or disconnect your account in the app.
