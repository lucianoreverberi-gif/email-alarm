Good to know

What is different on iPhone, explained clearly.

On iPhone it happens in two steps, and it is worth knowing which is which.

  • First the notification arrives. With its own sound, for a few seconds, and it respects silent mode and Focus. iOS does not let an app take over the screen on its own, so it will not open full screen over other apps or the Lock Screen.
  • Then the alarm rings, when you open it. Tap the notification and Email Alarm opens on the alert: the sender, the subject, the alarm it matched, and the tone repeating until you press Stop. With the app open the tone plays even if the ringer switch is silenced.
  • What you need. A compatible Gmail or Outlook account, an internet connection, and permission to receive notifications. Keep your account connected and the alarms you want to receive enabled.
  • If you do not hear it. Check your iPhone's volume, silent mode and Focus settings. Delivery also depends on connectivity and on email and notification services; immediate alerts cannot be guaranteed in every situation.
