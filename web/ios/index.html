Ready in three steps

01

Connect your account

Authorize Gmail or personal Hotmail/Outlook. Their mail apps do not need to be installed.

02

Create an alarm

Choose a sender, a word or phrase in the subject, and whether either condition or both must match.

03

Choose your sound

Give your alarm a name and choose a tone to recognize it. Test the sound and check recent activity to see which messages matched your alarms.

Choose the days and time window when each alarm can ring. Outside that schedule, it won't interrupt you. If it rings at a bad time, snooze it for 1, 5, or 10 minutes. You decide what deserves to interrupt you and when.

Sender and subject. No body or attachments.

Matches use the sender and subject line. We don't need to read the email body or attachments to apply these filters.

You authorize the connection through Google or Microsoft without entering your email password in Email Alarm. The service uses a server to check for matches and send alerts even when the app is not open. See the privacy policy for details about how data is handled and retained.

Good to know

What is different on iPhone, explained clearly.

On iPhone, the notification sound lasts a few seconds and respects silent mode and Focus. It does not automatically open a full-screen view over other apps or the Lock Screen. Tap the notification to open Email Alarm, see the alert details, and stop any alarm playing inside the app.

A compatible Gmail or Outlook account, an internet connection, and permission to receive notifications. Keep your account connected and the alarms you want to receive enabled.

To hear alerts, check your iPhone's volume, silent mode, and Focus settings. Delivery also depends on connectivity and email and notification services; immediate alerts cannot be guaranteed in every situation.
