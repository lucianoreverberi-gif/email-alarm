Good to know

What is different on iPhone, explained clearly.

On iPhone it happens in two steps, and it is worth knowing which is which.

The notification. It arrives with its own sound, lasts a few seconds and respects silent mode and Focus. iOS does not let an app take over the screen on its own, so it will not open full screen over other apps or the Lock Screen.

The alarm. Tap the notification and Email Alarm opens on the alert: the sender, the subject, the alarm it matched, and the tone repeating until you press Stop. With the app open the tone plays even if the ringer switch is silenced.

A compatible Gmail or Outlook account, an internet connection, and permission to receive notifications. Keep your account connected and the alarms you want to receive enabled.

To hear alerts, check your iPhone's volume, silent mode, and Focus settings. Delivery also depends on connectivity and email and notification services; immediate alerts cannot be guaranteed in every situation.
